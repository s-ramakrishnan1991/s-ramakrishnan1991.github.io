Qiskit Fall Fest 2025: PushQuantum Online Bootcamp#

IBM Quantum Event | 2025#

I deepened technical proficiency in quantum computing through an intensive bootcamp focused on the IBM Quantum ecosystem and the Qiskit framework.

Key Competencies Gained:#

1. Quantum Programming & Development: I Developed and executed quantum circuits using Python and Qiskit, gaining hands-on experience with the full development lifecycle.

2. Hardware Execution & Scaling: I mastered the process of submitting jobs to IBM Quantum backends and explored strategies for scaling algorithms to a larger number of qubits.

3. Error Management: I implemented error mitigation techniques to improve result fidelity on near-term noisy quantum devices (NISQ).

4. Quantum Assembly: Developed a working knowledge of OpenQASM for low-level quantum circuit representation.

5. Industry Insights: Engaged in specialized sessions on the quantum career landscape, identifying key entry paths and the multidisciplinary skill sets required for the evolving quantum industry.
