New Teams mean Tighter Grid

February 10, 2026

The 2026 season is special because the grid is growing! For the first time in many years, we have more than 10 teams. There are also big changes for the companies that build the engines.

1. Cadillac (The 11th Team)#

The biggest news for 2026 is the arrival of Cadillac. This is a famous American brand.

The Team: They are the 11th team on the grid. They are working with the Andretti family (a very famous name in racing).

The Engine: For now, they use engines from Ferrari. However, in a few years, they plan to build their own Cadillac engines.

The Drivers: They have some familiar faces! Sergio “Checo” Perez and Valtteri Bottas are driving for them this year.

2. Audi (A New Factory Team)#

You might remember a team called “Sauber” or “Kick Sauber.” That team is gone. It is now the Audi Factory Team.

What’s New: Audi bought the whole team. They built a brand-new engine in Germany specifically for these new rules.

The Goal: Audi is a huge company, and they want to win races quickly. They have hired Nico Hülkenberg and the young star Gabriel Bortoleto to lead them.

3. Red Bull Ford#

Red Bull is still here, but they have a new partner: Ford.

The Return: Ford is a massive American car company. They haven’t been in F1 for over 20 years!

The Job: They are helping Red Bull build their own engines (called Red Bull Powertrains). You will see the “Ford” logo on the championship-winning cars driven by Max Verstappen.

4. Honda & Aston Martin#

In the past, Honda made engines for Red Bull. Now, they have moved to Aston Martin.

A “Works” Team: This means Honda and Aston Martin work together as one single team.

The Legend: Fernando Alonso is still driving for Aston Martin, and he is very happy to work with Honda engines again.
