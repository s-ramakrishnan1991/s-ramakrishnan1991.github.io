## **QWorld: QBronze Online Entangling Workshop**
Through this intensive program, I transitioned from classical programming logic to quantum-native development using Python and Qiskit. I developed a basic foundation in quantum mechanics and the algorithmic structures that drive the next generation of computing.

### **Key Knowledge & Technical Milestones**
**1. Quantum Circuit Engineering**
Qiskit Integration: Mastered the workflow for designing, building, and executing quantum circuits on simulated environments.

State Manipulation: Developed an intuition for quantum superposition and the application of the Hadamard Operator to create probabilistic states.

**2. Geometric & Mathematical Foundations**
Real-Valued Qubit Logic: Gained a deep understanding of quantum operations as geometric transformations, specifically performing Rotations and Reflections on the Unit Circle.

State Verification: Learned to perform Quantum Tomography to measure and reconstruct unknown quantum states, ensuring the accuracy of quantum operations.

**3. Information Protocols & Entanglement**
Multi-Qubit Systems: Explored the mechanics of two-qubit gates and the creation of Quantum Entanglement.

Advanced Protocols: Successfully implemented communication frameworks including:

Quantum Teleportation: Moving quantum information across distances.

Superdense Coding: Using entangled pairs to transmit classical data with higher efficiency.

Phase Logic: Mastered Phase Kickback, a crucial mechanism for transferring information between registers in complex algorithms.

**4. Quantum Search & Optimization**
Grover’s Algorithm: Deepened my expertise in algorithmic speedups by implementing Grover’s Search.

Inversion About the Mean: Studied the mathematical amplification of probability amplitudes to find solutions in unstructured datasets efficiently.
