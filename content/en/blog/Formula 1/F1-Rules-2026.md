Welcome to the 2026 Formula 1 season! This year is very exciting because the cars have changed a lot. 
The new rules make the racing closer, the cars smaller, and the engines "greener."
Here is a simple guide to the biggest changes in F1 for 2026.

## **1. Smaller and Lighter Cars**

In the past few years, F1 cars were very big and heavy. For 2026, the FIA (the people who make the rules) decided to make them "nimble." 
This means they are easier to move and better for racing in tight spaces.

**Weight:** The cars are 30kg lighter.

**Size:** The cars are shorter and narrower.

**Tyres:** The tyres are also a bit smaller to save weight.

## **2. Active Aerodynamics (Wings that move!)**

In the past, drivers used "DRS" to open the back wing and go faster. Now, the cars have Active Aerodynamics. The front and rear wings can change shape during the race.

**Z-Mode:** This is for corners. The wings stay open to give the car more "grip" so it doesn't slide.

**X-Mode:** This is for the long straights. The wings flatten out to make the car go much faster through the air.

## **3. A New Type of Engine**
The 2026 engines still use a V6 Turbo, but the "battery power" is now much more important.

**More Electricity:** Almost half of the car's power now comes from the electric battery.

**100% Green Fuel:** The cars no longer use normal petrol. They use a special sustainable fuel that does not hurt the environment.

**Cheaper to Build:** The engines are now simpler so that new companies (like Audi and Ford) can join the sport.

## **4. How Overtaking Works**
Since every car can move its wings now, F1 added a new way to overtake called Manual Override Mode.
If a driver is close to the car in front, they get an extra "boost" of electricity. 
The car in front will lose some power when it goes very fast, but the driver behind can use their battery to keep accelerating. It is like a "boost button" in a video game!
